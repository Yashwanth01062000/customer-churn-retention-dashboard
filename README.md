# Customer Churn & Retention Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes customer churn behavior and retention trends using key business metrics and interactive visualizations.

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and business growth. This dashboard helps identify churn patterns, customer risk indicators, and retention opportunities.

## Key KPIs

- Total Customers
- Churn Rate %
- Revenue at Risk
- Average Tenure

## Visualizations

- Customer Retention vs Churn Overview
- Churn Rate by Product Usage Level
- Impact of Support Issues on Churn
- Customer Retention by Subscription Plan
- Customer Risk Matrix
- Churn Rate Across Customer Tenure
